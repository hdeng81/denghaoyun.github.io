I am a fifth year Ph.D. student in Operations Research at the H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering at Georgia Tech, advised by Dr. Nick Sahinidis and Dr. Weijun Xie.

Before joining Georgia Tech, I received a B.S. with honors in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics and completed a second major in Computer Science and Technology at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics.

Research Interests

Optimization for complex systems under uncertainty and nonconvexity

I am broadly interested in optimization and decision-making under uncertainty. My research develops scalable computational methods and mathematical models for challenging problems involving uncertainty, discrete decisions, and nonconvexity. My work spans stochastic programming, integer programming, nonconvex optimization, and bilevel optimization. I am particularly motivated by applications in transportation systems, with a current focus on air mobility planning.
